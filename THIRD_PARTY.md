# Third-party runtime inputs

The add-on is exact-gated to the four artifacts listed in `README.md` and reads
their installed JSON, OBJ/MTL and texture resources at BlueMap load time.
Mekanism Core, Generators and MoreMachine declare MIT; Mekanism Covers declares
MPL-2.0. No Covers source is adapted and no third-party resources are bundled.
